Opportunity intelligence, without the fake certainty.

Cashh Radar is a decision-support system for finding realistic ways to earn. It collects opportunities from configured sources, preserves source links and observation dates, separates verified records from stale or illustrative ones, and ranks options using practical factors such as speed, accessibility, startup cost, earning potential, durability, competition and risk.

Important: Cashh Radar does not guarantee earnings, acceptance, eligibility or future availability. A ranking is an estimate designed to help decide what to investigate and do next.

What does Cashh Radar verify?

Source identity
Canonical source URL and source record identifiers when available.
Freshness
First-seen, last-seen, closing dates and stale/expired states.
Evidence
Source facts are kept distinct from estimates, modeled scores and promotional claims.
Risk
Restrictions, eligibility, suspicious signals and confidence are part of the decision.

How are opportunities ranked?

Cashh Radar combines a general quality score with user-specific fit. The current model considers speed to income, income potential, accessibility, durability, startup cost, competition and verification/risk. A user's work mode, budget, urgency, experience and stated goal can change the ordering.

What kinds of opportunities can it track?

The data model supports employment, remote work, freelance projects, grants, business opportunities and other legitimate income paths. A source must still be evaluated on its own terms. “Available on the internet” is not the same thing as “safe, current or a good fit,” a distinction the internet occasionally forgets with impressive dedication.

What do the trust states mean?

Verified means the record received an explicit verification step. Source confirmed means it was recently observed from a configured source. Stale means the last observation exceeded its freshness window. Expired means its closing date passed. Illustrative means it is a demonstration/example and must not be presented as a live opportunity.

How should someone use Cashh Radar?

  1. Describe the goal and constraints.
  2. Review the highest-ranked current opportunities.
  3. Open the source evidence before acting.
  4. Check eligibility, deadlines, costs and payment terms.
  5. Turn the best option into a concrete roadmap or outreach/application action.
  6. Track the outcome so later rankings can be judged against reality.

Does Cashh Radar sell “guaranteed income”?

No. Any income figure can depend on acceptance, hours, performance, geography, demand, taxes, fees, market conditions and other variables. The product is designed to make uncertainty visible rather than disguise it as a sales slogan.
