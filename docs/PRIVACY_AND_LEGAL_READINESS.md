# Cashh Radar Privacy & Legal Readiness — v2.2

Public routes now exist for:

- `/privacy`
- `/terms`
- `/disclosures`
- `/security`
- `/support`

They provide a launch baseline covering account/product data, user controls, evidence/estimate disclosures, outcome disclaimers, referral/provider compensation, security controls and a configurable support contact.

Software controls include:

- JSON data export;
- password change/reset;
- email verification and optional two-step email login;
- safe permanent account deletion;
- owner-transfer guard for Team workspaces;
- minimal first-party analytics;
- auditable referrals/provider workflow;
- evidence labels and explicit illustrative records.

These pages are not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Before aggressive paid marketing, affiliate expansion, or operation in regulated/specialized jurisdictions, have the policies reviewed for the actual company/entity, address, regions served, data processors, refund policy and commercial relationships.
