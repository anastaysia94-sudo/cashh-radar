### Directory description
Cashh Radar is an evidence-labeled opportunity intelligence and action platform designed to turn scattered opportunities into a usable decision queue. It can organize client prospects alongside broader money, work, business, job, grant, and contract opportunities while preserving the source and freshness information behind each result.

Instead of treating every listing as equally trustworthy or equally useful, Cashh Radar tracks evidence state, source freshness, fit, urgency, deliverability, and user constraints. The system then helps users move from discovery to verification, prioritization, action, response tracking, outcome recording, and learning from what actually happened.

The platform includes an Opportunity Radar, Fast Money and Remote Income views, saved searches, watchlists, alerts and digests, an Opportunity Advisor, comparison tools, outreach assets, outcome tracking, referrals, team features, and a scoped commercial API. Its prospect workflow keeps individual review before outreach and is designed around public or business-intended contact evidence rather than invented contact details.

Cashh Radar deliberately separates modeled value from realized results. Proposed offer value can be used for prioritization, but actual earnings are only treated as realized after the user records a real outcome. The goal is simple: help people spend less time searching, understand why an opportunity may fit, and move the strongest options into action without pretending uncertain information is guaranteed.

The app brings different kinds of opportunities into one workflow, keeps the source and freshness information attached, and helps rank the strongest matches against real constraints. It also tracks what happens after you act, so future prioritization can learn from actual outcomes instead of imagined results.

I’m looking for feedback on where the workflow is useful, confusing, or missing something important. If you try it, criticism is more useful than empty hype.

### Builder-community post
I’ve been building Cashh Radar as an evidence-first opportunity engine rather than another giant list of links. The core loop is: discover → verify → rank → act → record the outcome → learn.

The part I care about most is keeping estimates and real outcomes separate. If the app models a possible offer value, it stays labeled as a model. It does not become “earnings” unless a real outcome is recorded.

I’d especially value feedback from builders who have worked on ranking systems, job/opportunity discovery, lead workflows, or evidence freshness.
