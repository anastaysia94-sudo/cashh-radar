# Cashh Radar Commercial Offer Ladder

Updated: 2026-09-30

Cashh Radar should sell a progression of increasingly hands-on value instead of unrelated offers.

## Customer path

### 1. Free intelligence
Use Cashh Radar public discovery, status, educational content, and evidence-first explanations to help a visitor understand the opportunity/problem before asking for money.

Purpose: earn attention and trust without pretending a sale or result already exists.

### 2. Self-serve digital products
Private customer-ready packages have been extracted from the working Cashh Radar system:

- Opportunity Scoring Kit
- Prospecting Command Pack
- Evidence-First Income Planner
- Founder Revenue Experiment Workbook
- Opportunity Intelligence Brief Template

The source ZIPs are intentionally kept outside this public repository. Storefront selling prices must be explicitly confirmed before publication.

Purpose: serve buyers who want the system/template but not implementation.

### 3. Entry Fix Desk — $99–$100
Current live offers:

- AI Copy Cleanup — $99
- CRM / Spreadsheet Rescue — $99
- Business Content Pack — $100

The $100 Business Content Pack personalized mobile path has passed live PWA and PayPal-handoff E2E verification.

Purpose: low-friction first paid implementation.

### 4. Growth Fix Desk — $149
Current live offers:

- AI Search Visibility Starter — $149
- Website Conversion Rescue — $149
- Lead Research Mini-Sprint — $149

Purpose: solve a more commercially meaningful problem with a bounded same-day-target scope.

### 5. Local Growth — $199
Current live offer:

- Local Business Quick-Win Pack — $199

Purpose: combine visibility, review, conversion, and prioritization work into one larger fixed scope.

### 6. Launch Rescue — $249
Current live offer:

- Emergency Launch Bundle — $249

Purpose: highest-value fixed-scope intervention for several launch leaks at once.

## Cross-sell logic

Opportunity Scoring Kit → Opportunity Intelligence Brief / Cashh Radar.
Prospecting Command Pack → Lead Research Mini-Sprint / Business Content Pack.
Evidence-First Income Planner → recurring Cashh Radar monitoring when real billing is activated.
Founder Revenue Experiment Workbook → Website Conversion Rescue / Emergency Launch Bundle.
Opportunity Intelligence Brief Template → research/implementation service or future Team/API use.

## Integrity rules

- No guaranteed revenue, ranking, customer, reply, or conversion claims.
- Modeled value is never labeled realized money.
- A checkout click is not a purchase.
- An invoice is not revenue until payment is actually received.
- Digital product source files remain private until storefront delivery is configured.
- Recurring, Team, API, or enterprise offers are not labeled active until their real billing/provisioning path is verified.
