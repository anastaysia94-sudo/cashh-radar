# Cashh Radar Digital Product Catalog

Updated: 2026-09-30
Brand attribution: SmartPickShop Holdings

These are productized derivatives of systems already used inside Cashh Radar. The premium source files are not stored in this public repository.

## Opportunity Scoring Kit
Buyer problem: too many opportunities and no evidence-based way to choose.
Included customer assets: opportunity scorecard, evidence ledger, decision rubric, license/readme.
Primary cross-sell: Opportunity Intelligence Brief or Cashh Radar.

## Prospecting Command Pack
Buyer problem: prospecting becomes an unmeasured spreadsheet-and-email mess.
Included customer assets: prospect tracker, ethical outreach sequence, value-per-hour calculator, license/readme.
Primary cross-sell: Lead Research Mini-Sprint or Business Content Pack.

## Evidence-First Income Planner
Buyer problem: forecasts and hoped-for earnings get confused with money actually received.
Included customer assets: income experiment tracker, weekly summary, modeled-versus-realized guide, license/readme.
Primary cross-sell: recurring monitoring when activated.

## Founder Revenue Experiment Workbook
Buyer problem: founders run random marketing activity without a clean experiment/result loop.
Included customer assets: experiment log, funnel metrics, offer-test card, guardrails, license/readme.
Primary cross-sell: Website Conversion Rescue or Emergency Launch Bundle.

## Opportunity Intelligence Brief Template
Buyer problem: research is scattered, unsourced, or hard to turn into an action decision.
Included customer assets: brief structure, source log, action plan, handoff checklist, license/readme.
Primary cross-sell: research/implementation service or future Team/API use.
